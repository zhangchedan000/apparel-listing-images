# 提示词片段

## 一、提示词组成
每条生图提示词 = 本张画面 + 【商品描述】+ 【视角】+ 【真实感】+ 【单图】+ 【规格】
用通顺的英文句子写，不用逗号堆关键词。附什么参考图按 SKILL.md 第四节的表格。

**【商品描述】** 档案里的英文商品描述原样放入，包括图案形状和"不是什么样"，例：
> A men's navy blue hooded jacket. Matte navy outer fabric with evenly spaced short diagonal embossed dashes all over (NOT chevron or V-shaped quilting seams). Cream sherpa lining. Black front zipper, navy drawstrings with silver metal tips, one vertical black zip pocket on each side, navy ribbed cuffs and hem. No logo.

第 1 张附原始上传图时加：
> Copy the DESIGN of the garment in the attached photo exactly: color, color blocking and contrast panels, trims, pocket shape and position, zippers, zipper tapes and their labels, drawstrings, hardware and ribbing. Do not add, remove, simplify or redesign any of these. Do NOT copy the photo's flat shape, wrinkles, draping, lighting or camera angle; re-shape the garment fully in the presentation style described below, on a pure white background.

第 3 张起附主参考图时加：
> The attached image is the master reference. Keep the garment's appearance, colors, details and presentation style exactly the same. Only change what this image requires (view angle, crop or layout).

深色商品加：
> Keep the original brightness and contrast so the fabric texture, seams and folds stay clearly visible; avoid crushed blacks.

**【视角】**
- 正面：`Straight-on front view, the camera faces the garment directly at chest height, symmetrical left to right, not turned or angled.`
- 背面：`Rear view, the camera is directly behind the garment. Only the back of the garment is visible: <按档案填实际有的背面结构，如 back panel / back of the hood / backs of the sleeves / ribbed hem>. Front-only details such as <按档案填：zipper / drawstrings / front pockets / lining> are NOT visible.`
- 侧面图：`Side profile view at 90 degrees`；3/4 图：`Three-quarter view at 45 degrees`；再加一句按档案填的轮廓特征（如有帽子写 hood silhouette，没有就不写）
- 括号里只填档案里真实存在的结构，不存在的不写
- 第 2 张，纯白在前时（亚马逊、TK+合规）编辑第 1 张加元素：`Edit the attached image. Keep the garment, its position, size, angle and the white background exactly unchanged. Only add the following elements in the empty corners: …`
- 编辑实拍图（背面等）：`Edit the attached photo. Keep the garment's shape, angle, fabric texture and every detail exactly unchanged. Only replace the background with pure white (#FFFFFF), even out the lighting to soft studio light, and output a vertical 3:4 image.`
- 尺码图（编辑主参考图或底图）：`Use the attached front product image exactly as it is. Do not redraw or alter the garment. Add dashed measurement arrows with labels on the garment, a title, and a size table.` 再按版式加一句：
  - 上下结构：`Place the garment at the top and the size table below.`
  - 左右结构：`Place the garment on the left and the title and size table on the right.`
- ③ 的尺码底图：附主参考图，`The same garment as in the attached image, shown alone on an invisible ghost mannequin, straight-on front view, pure white background, no person, no text.`
- 第 2 张，加元素在前时（TK）编辑第 1 张去元素：`Edit the attached image. Remove all added graphics, icons, circles and text. Keep the garment, its position, size and angle exactly unchanged, on a pure white background.`

**【真实感】**
> Photorealistic commercial product photography shot on a professional DSLR camera, true-to-life colors, natural fabric texture with realistic wrinkles and thickness. No CGI or 3D render look, no plastic or over-smoothed appearance.

隐形模特（①④ 和尺码底图）加：`Invisible ghost mannequin effect, the garment looks naturally filled out as if worn, no mannequin visible.`

3D 立体（②）加，按品类选一句：
- 裤子：`3D ghost mannequin product photo. The pants are fully filled out as if worn by an invisible body: round, cylindrical legs with real volume, the waistband opening visible from a slightly elevated angle showing the inside of the waistband, natural vertical folds and soft shadows that follow the body shape. Not a flat lay, not a flat product cutout.`
- 上衣 / 外套：`3D ghost mannequin product photo. The garment is fully filled out as if worn by an invisible body: rounded chest and shoulders, tube-shaped sleeves, the neckline or hood opening showing its inside, natural folds and soft shadows that follow the body shape. Not a flat lay, not a flat product cutout.`
模特加：`Natural realistic skin texture, normal body proportions, anatomically correct hands and fingers.` 第一张模特图之后再加上回复里写好的英文模特描述

**【单图】**
> A single photograph, one frame only. No grid, no collage, no split screen, no multiple panels, no borders.

卖点图 / 尺码图（画面里本来就有特写或表格）改用：
> One single image with one unified layout. Do not create multiple separate images or a collage of different photos.

**【规格】**
> Highest available resolution and quality, target 1600×2133 px or larger, vertical 3:4.

纯白正面图加（**带元素的那张不加这一句**）：`Pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255). No text, logo, watermark, badges, graphics or props. The garment is fully visible and fills about 85% of the frame.`
