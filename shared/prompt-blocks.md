# 固定英文提示词片段

每条生图提示词 = 【本张画面描述】+【商品锁定】+【真实感约束】+【单图约束】+【规格】
用通顺的英文句子写，不要用逗号堆关键词。

## 商品锁定
> Use the attached product photo as the exact reference. Keep the garment identical: same color, fabric texture, stitching, zipper, pockets, drawstrings, cuffs and hem. Do not add, remove or redesign any part of the garment. Do not add logos or graphics that are not on the original.

## 真实感约束
> Photorealistic commercial product photography shot on a professional DSLR camera, true-to-life colors, natural fabric texture with realistic wrinkles and thickness. No CGI or 3D render look, no plastic or over-smoothed appearance.

真人模特额外加：
> The model has natural, realistic skin texture and normal body proportions, with anatomically correct hands and fingers.

## 单图约束
> A single photograph, one frame only. No grid, no collage, no split screen, no multiple panels, no inset images, no borders.

（卖点图 / 尺码图本身包含圆圈特写或表格时，改为：）
> One single image with one unified layout. Do not create multiple separate images, grids or a collage of different photos.

## 规格
> Vertical 3:4 aspect ratio. Pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255).（背景按模板和平台调整）

## 主图额外
> No text, no logo, no watermark, no badges, no graphics, no props. The product fills about 85% of the frame and is fully visible, not cropped.
